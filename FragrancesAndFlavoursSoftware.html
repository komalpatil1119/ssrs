Fragrances And Flavours Software

Our Fragrances & Flavours Management Software is a specialized ERP solution designed for aroma, flavour, and essence manufacturers. It streamlines the complete business lifecycle—from formulation and raw material management to production, quality compliance, costing, and sales—ensuring consistency, traceability, and regulatory compliance.

Built for R&D-driven and batch-based industries, the system helps organizations maintain formulation secrecy, optimize inventory usage, control batch quality, and scale operations efficiently while meeting global standards.

Key Capabilities

Specialized ERP capabilities designed for fragrance and flavour manufacturers, ensuring formulation accuracy, quality compliance, and complete operational control.

Formula & Recipe Management

Create, store, and manage fragrance and flavour formulations with version control, ingredient ratios, and secure approval workflows.

Raw Material & Inventory Control

Track aroma chemicals, essential oils, solvents, and additives with batch, lot, expiry, and storage-condition tracking.

Batch Production Management

Plan and monitor batch-wise production with yield tracking, process stages, rework handling, and wastage control.

Quality Control & Compliance

Manage in-process and final quality checks, COA, MSDS, IFRA, FSSAI, and regulatory documentation.

Costing & Margin Analysis

Calculate product costing based on raw material prices, batch size, process losses, and overheads.

Customer & Order Management

Manage customer enquiries, samples, custom formulations, sales orders, and repeat order history.

Sample & R&D Tracking

Track lab samples, trial batches, R&D iterations, customer approvals, and formulation changes.

Reports & Business Analytics

Real-time dashboards for production efficiency, inventory valuation, batch performance, sales trends, and profitability.

Our Features

Advanced ERP features designed to protect formulations, ensure compliance, and optimize fragrance and flavour manufacturing operations.

Formula Secrecy & Role-based Access
Batch & Lot-wise Traceability
