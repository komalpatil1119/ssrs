Cables Marketing & Production System

Our Cables Marketing & Production System is an end-to-end ERP solution designed for cable manufacturers and marketers. It integrates production planning, inventory control, sales, and financial management into one centralized platform—helping you reduce waste, improve efficiency, and boost profitability.

What we offer -
  • Production Planning & Control
  • Raw Material & Inventory Management
  • Product Configuration & BOM Management
  • Sales & Marketing Management
  • Dispatch & Logistics Management
  • Quality Control & Compliance
  • Accounting & Financial Management
  • Reports & Business Analytics

Why Choose Our Cables ERP System?

Our Cables ERP System is purpose-built for cable manufacturing and marketing businesses, offering complete control over production, inventory, and sales operations. It ensures accurate tracking of raw materials, lengths, drums, and finished goods while minimizing wastage and operational errors.

  • ✔ Designed specifically for cable manufacturing & marketing.
  • ✔ Accurate length, weight & drum tracking.
  • ✔ Reduces material wastage & production costs.
  • ✔ GST-ready & compliance-focused.
  • ✔ Scalable for single or multi-plant operations.
  • ✔ Dedicated implementation & support team.
