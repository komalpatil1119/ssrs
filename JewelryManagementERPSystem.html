Jewellery Management ERP System

Our Jewellery Management ERP System is a comprehensive, industry-specific solution designed to streamline and digitize the complete jewellery business lifecycle. Built for manufacturers, wholesalers, retailers, and exporters, the system manages everything from customer orders and inventory to production, costing, and compliance.

By integrating sales, design, manufacturing, inventory, accounting, and quality control into a single platform, the ERP ensures accuracy, transparency, and real-time control over precious metals, gemstones, and finished jewellery. It helps businesses reduce losses, improve operational efficiency, and maintain complete traceability across gold, silver, diamond, and gemstone operations.

Key Capabilities

Comprehensive ERP capabilities designed to manage jewellery operations with precision, transparency, and complete business control.

Order & Customer Management

Manage customer enquiries, customized orders, repair jobs, and order tracking with complete customer history.

Design & Product Configuration

Handle jewellery designs, variants, sizes, weights, and gemstone specifications with detailed product masters.

Metal & Stone Inventory Control

Track gold, silver, platinum, diamonds, and gemstones with purity, weight, lot, and location-wise accuracy.

Production & Job Work Management

Monitor manufacturing stages, karigar/job-worker assignments, WIP tracking, and wastage control.

Costing & Pricing Management

Calculate jewellery costing based on metal rates, stone value, making charges, wastage, and margins.

Barcode & Tagging System

Enable barcode or RFID tagging for jewellery items to ensure fast billing, stock accuracy, and traceability.

Compliance & Hallmark Management

Manage hallmarking, certification, regulatory documentation, and maintain complete audit-ready records.

Reports & Business Analytics

Access real-time reports on stock valuation, sales, margins, metal movement, and overall business performance.

Powerful ERP features designed to manage jewellery operations with accuracy, transparency, and complete business control.
